# Roundi — Support

Roundi is a visual focus timer for iPhone, iPad and Android by Yakppal, Inc.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports, feedback, or data requests: **support@yakppal.com**

We usually reply within two business days. When reporting a problem, it helps to
include your device model, OS version and the app version (Settings, bottom of the page).

## Common questions

**A round ended when I didn't expect it.**
Leaving the app, locking the screen or opening system panels (like Notification
Center) before the round ends finishes it. Turn on *Lenient Mode* in Settings if you
prefer rounds that keep running.

**Where is my history stored?**
Only on your device. There is no account and no Roundi cloud sync; your rounds are
included in your phone's own iCloud or Google backup, if you use one.
