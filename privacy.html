Privacy Policy

Roundi · Version 1.0 · Last updated: September 11, 2026

Roundi is a visual focus timer made by Yakppal, Inc. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: your data stays on your device. We collect nothing.

What the app stores

Roundi keeps your timer rounds (start time, duration, outcome, optional tag) and your settings (colors, sounds, tags) in a local database on your device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Roundi has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. The app does not transmit your data to Yakppal or to any third party.

Device backups

Because your rounds are stored on your device, they may be included in your device's own backup (iCloud on iOS, Google backup on Android). Those backups are governed by Apple's or Google's terms and your device settings, not by us.

Permissions

Roundi asks for notification permission only, to play a chime and show an alert when your round ends. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. You can decline or revoke this permission at any time in system settings; the timer still works.

Children

Roundi does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device (a copy may persist in your device backup until that backup is replaced, per your device's backup settings).

Changes

If a future version of Roundi ever collects data — for example, if we add optional ads or subscriptions — we will update this policy first and note the change here, with a new version number and date.
